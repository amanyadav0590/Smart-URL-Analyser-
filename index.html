Online Safety Tips

Stay aware before opening any link

🔒 1. Check HTTPS

HTTPS encrypts the connection, but it does not guarantee that a website is trustworthy.

🔍 2. Check the Domain

Carefully check spelling and look for misleading domain names.

⚠️ 3. Avoid Unknown Links

Avoid opening links received from unknown people or suspicious messages.

🔑 4. Protect Your Password

Never enter your password or OTP on an unfamiliar website.

🛡️ 5. Verify Website Identity

Check the official website address before logging in or making payments.
